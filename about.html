About privacytracker.

A small privacykey project, built to close the gap between what apps say on the App Store and what they actually do on your phone.

privacykey

privacytracker is a privacykey project. The maintainer is Adam, with contributors visible on GitHub.

We built it to close a small but important gap. Every iOS app has a privacy label on the App Store — a checklist of what it collects and what it does with that data. Those labels can change at any time, often quietly, and the developer privacy policies they reference are written for lawyers rather than the people who actually use the apps. So most people never read them, and even those who do can't easily tell when something has shifted.

privacytracker fills that gap. It checks the labels for you, translates each policy into plain English, and tells you when something changes. That's the whole product.

The app is free, open source under Apache 2.0, and runs entirely on your computer — there is no privacytracker server, no account, no telemetry. The code is at github.com/privacykey/privacytracker.

For bugs or feature ideas, open an issue. For security concerns, please follow SECURITY.md instead of a public report.
